# Contributor Covenant — Code of Conduct (csn)

## Our intent

A small, technical, safety-focused community. Discussion should stay concrete: evidence, threats, consent, code. The maintainers are protective of contribution quality but the tone stays professional and courteous.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code and designs, not people.
- Assume reviewers ask "is this honestly gated?" to protect users, not to gatekeep you.
- Keep discussions of security flaws within coordinated channels (see SECURITY.md).
- Respect the project's hard boundary (no covert monitoring features); arguing for it politely is fine, implementing it in PRs is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, ad-hominems, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing), including mapping a CSN user's identity from capability reports.
- Deliberately shipping secret-content into issues, docs, or fixtures.
- Spamming bugs related to products CSN merely *reads configs of* (OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) instead of their proper upstreams.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, in order: a comment, a thread, PR/issue rights, and finally, for repeated severe violations, repo access. Decisions are documented in the moderation log (storage location announced atop the repo when public). To report conduct issues, use the maintainers' contact listed in the repo profile.
